FORMULA

Δg = GM(1/r₁² − 1/r₂²)

Assumptions

Required: M>0 and r₂≥r₁>0, with both points on the same outward radial ray and outside a spherical body. Inputs are center distances. This app restricts weak-field use to r_s/r₁≤0.01. The output is a differential acceleration, not force, stress or a three-dimensional tidal tensor. Excluded: rotation, other bodies and structural response.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
